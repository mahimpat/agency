Questions

Good to know.

Who writes the letters?

Letters are written with the help of AI, in each pen pal's voice, from a verified fact sheet for every figure. A separate fact-checking step reviews each letter before it's printed, and anything imagined (like a letter travelling through time) is clearly marked on the "Real history" card. You can read every letter before it's posted.

What ages is it for?

Ages 5–12. Letters match your child's reading level: shorter, with simple words, for young children (great for reading aloud), and richer for older children. Written in British English.

How does writing back work?

Your child writes on any paper, or on the back of the letter. You take a photo and upload it, or type it in. The next letter answers their questions and responds to their news.

What about difficult history?

Every pen pal has age-appropriate guidelines, and a few are kept for children aged 8 and over. Letters are warm and encouraging, and you can tell us about any topics you'd like us to avoid.

What about privacy?

We collect information from parents only, never directly from children, and only what we need to write and post the letters. Photos of replies are deleted as soon as they've been read. Read our privacy policy.

Where do you post to?

UK addresses, with letters printed and posted in the UK by Royal Mail 2nd class. More countries may follow: email us if you'd like to know when we reach yours.
