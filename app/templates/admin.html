Safeguarding steps (full policy: docs/safeguarding.md)
  1. Immediate danger to a child: call 999 now.
  2. Worried about harm, abuse or neglect: contact the child's local council children's services (find them at gov.uk/report-child-abuse-to-local-council). You can also get advice from the NSPCC helpline: 0808 800 5000.

  4. Never reply to the child about a disclosure yourself, and never promise to keep a secret.
  5. Write down what you saw, what you did and when, in the reply's note in your records.
