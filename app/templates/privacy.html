Privacy policy

Last updated: {{ POLICY_DATE }}

{{ PRODUCT }} sends children real letters from historical figures. To do that we need some information about you and your child. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights. We've tried to write it plainly.

The short version

  • We collect information from parents, never directly from children. Children don't have accounts.
  • We only ask for what the letters need: your child's first name, age, interests, and where to post the letters.
  • Photos of your child's replies are deleted as soon as they've been read. We keep only the words, so the next letter can answer them. The one exception is a reply held for a safety review (section 4), whose photo is kept until a person has reviewed it.
  • Letters are written with the help of AI. The AI is told your child's first name, age and interests, never their surname or address.
  • We never sell your data, and we never use it for advertising.
  • You can download or delete everything from your account at any time.

4. Your child's information

We take extra care with information about children:

  • Parents provide it, not children. Only a parent or guardian, or someone with their permission, can sign a child up. Children never create accounts or log in.
  • Only what's needed. We ask for a first name, age and interests. We don't need, and don't ask for, a surname for the letters themselves, a date of birth, a school, or photos of your child.
  • Letters never ask children for personal details. Pen pals ask about ideas, hobbies and opinions, never addresses, schools or photos. Please help your child leave personal details out of their replies.
  • Reply photos are deleted as soon as they've been read, normally within a day of upload. We keep the written-out words, with personal details such as addresses, phone numbers and email addresses automatically removed, so the next letter can answer them.
  • Safety. Every reply goes through two independent safety checks: an AI safeguarding assessment, and a rule-based screen for worrying words. If either suggests a child might be unhappy, unsafe or at risk, no automatic answer is written and a member of our team reads it. We may contact you. If we believe a child is at risk of harm, we will contact the appropriate authorities (such as the local council's children's services, or 999 in an emergency). We keep a held reply's photo, and a record of what we did, for as long as needed to protect the child.
  • No profiling for marketing, no advertising, no selling.

5. How AI is used

Letters are written with the help of an AI system (Claude, made by Anthropic), using a verified fact sheet for each historical figure. A separate AI check reviews every letter for accuracy and child safety, and a member of our team approves letters before they're posted.

  • To write a letter, the AI receives your child's first name, age, interests, things to avoid, earlier letters, and the words of their reply. It does not receive their surname, your name, your email, or your address.
  • To read a handwritten reply, the photo you upload is sent to the AI to write out the words. The photo is then deleted from our systems.
  • Anthropic processes this information only to provide the service to us, as our processor. Under its commercial terms, it does not use our customers' information to train its models.
  • The AI does not make decisions about you or your child that have legal or similarly significant effects.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your information. Download it any time from your account, or ask us;
  • correct anything that's wrong;
  • delete your information. Delete your account from your account page, or ask us;
  • restrict or object to how we use it, including at any time for news and offers;
  • take your information elsewhere (data portability);
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it.

As a parent or guardian you can use these rights on your child's behalf. Older children can also contact us themselves. Email {{ contact_email }}; we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, 0303 123 1113.

10. Cookies

We use one strictly necessary cookie to keep you logged in and to protect forms from misuse. It's needed for the site to work, so it doesn't need consent. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. If we count visitors, we use a privacy-friendly service that doesn't set cookies or identify you.

11. Keeping it safe

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), store passwords only as secure one-way hashes, limit who on our team can see information, and delete reply photos as soon as they've been read. No system is perfectly secure. If something goes wrong that affects your information, we'll tell you and the ICO where the law requires.
