Questions

Who writes the letters?

Letters are written with the help of AI, in each figure's voice, from a verified fact sheet for every figure. A separate fact-checking step reviews each letter before it's printed, and anything imagined (like letters traveling through time) is clearly marked on the "Real history" card.

What ages is it for?

Ages 5–12. Letters match your child's reading level: shorter with simple words for young children (great for reading aloud), richer for older kids.

How does writing back work?

Your child writes on any paper, or on the back of the letter. You snap a photo and upload it. The next letter answers their questions and responds to their news.

What about difficult history?

Every figure has age-appropriate guidelines. Letters are warm and encouraging, and you can tell us about topics you'd like us to avoid.

What about privacy?

We collect information from parents only, never directly from children, and only what we need to write and mail the letters. Reply photos are deleted after they're read. We never ask your child for personal details.

Where do you ship?

We're starting in the United States. Join the waitlist from anywhere: it helps us decide where to go next.
