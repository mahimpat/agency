# Marketing and Launch Plan: Letters from History

*Forem Innovations · October 2026 to March 2027 · Version 1*

> **The plan in one line:** open to founding families in mid-November, use Christmas to sell gift subscriptions (a printable certificate under the tree, first letter in January), then grow through home-education communities, World Book Day and Mothering Sunday.

All figures are estimates or targets to replace with real data as soon as we have it.

---

## 1. Goals

| By | Goal |
|---|---|
| 15 Nov 2026 | Product live; **30 founding families** from our own networks and the waitlist |
| 31 Dec 2026 | **100 paying subscribers + 75 gift subscriptions sold** |
| 31 Mar 2027 | **300 subscribers** |

**Health targets to watch every week:**

- **Customer acquisition cost (CAC):** at most **£25**, averaged across all channels. A Pen Pal subscriber is worth roughly £9.50 a month in margin, so £25 is paid back in about 3 months.
- **Reply rate:** **at least 40%** of Pen Pal children write back within a month. **This is the number that matters most.** A child who writes back stays subscribed.
- **Cancellations:** at most **8% a month**.
- **Gifts that convert:** at least **25%** of gift subscriptions become paid subscriptions when the gift ends.

**Budget:** about **£3,000** over five months (section 8).

## 3. Positioning and message

**Tagline:** *History writes back.*

**Three proof points** for every page, post and pitch:

1. **It's personal.** Every letter is written for one child, about the things they love.
2. **It answers back.** When a child writes, their pen pal answers their actual questions. We haven't found any other UK service that does this.
3. **It's true and safe.** Facts come from verified fact sheets, every letter is checked by people, and there's a Real history card on the back.

**On AI, be open and confident, never defensive:**

- **Say:** "Written with the help of AI, from verified historical facts, and checked by our team before posting."
- **Never** hide it, and never say "AI-generated letters" as the headline. The headline is the child's experience.

**Common questions and our answers:**

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Is it real history?" | Every fact comes from a verified fact sheet. The Real history card on the back shows what's true and what's storytelling. |
| "Isn't it just AI?" | AI helps us write a personal letter for every child. People check every letter before it's posted. |
| "Is it safe for my child?" | Children have no accounts and no contact with anyone. Letters never ask for personal details. Every reply goes through safety checks, and anything worrying is read by a trained person. |
| "What if my child doesn't write back?" | The story keeps going. Pen pals send follow-up letters, and many children start writing after a letter or two. |
| "£12 a month?" | That's two personalised letters, printed and posted, with replies answered: about £6 a letter. Story Letters is £7. |

## 4. The launch offer

| Offer | Details | Build needed? |
|---|---|---|
| **Founding families** | 30% off for life (Pen Pal £8.40, Story £4.90). Stripe promotion code `FOUNDING30`, set to last "forever", limited to the first 100 | **No.** Set up in Stripe |
| **Love it or it's free** | If you don't love the first letter, we refund the first month. It costs us about £2.44 at most and removes the risk of trying | **No.** Refund in Stripe |
| **Gift subscriptions** | 3 months £36 · 6 months £69 · 12 months £132. Buyer gets a **printable certificate** straight away; the family redeems it online and the first letter arrives in January | **Yes**: gift checkout, redemption code, certificate PDF (about 1-2 days of building) |
| **Give a month, get a month** | Referral reward after a family's second month | Later (January) |

**Why gift certificates are the core of the Christmas plan:**

- We avoid Royal Mail's Christmas peak. The last recommended 2nd class posting date is usually around **17-18 December** (check Royal Mail's published 2026 dates in November).
- People can buy right up to **24 December**.
- January letters arrive just as the Christmas excitement fades, which is good for keeping families subscribed.

## 5. Channels, in priority order

### 1. Our own networks and the waitlist (free, from now)

- Send the sample pack and landing page to friends, family and colleagues with children aged 5 to 12, and ask each person to forward it to two families.
- Post real letters to 5-10 children now (printed at home, 91p stamp). **Film the moment they open a reply**, with parents' written consent. That clip is our most important marketing asset.
- **Target:** 30 founding families by 15 November.

### 2. Home-education communities (free, from mid-November)

- Join 10-15 UK home-ed Facebook groups. Post only on promotion days and follow each group's rules.
- Lead with the experience: a child's question and the pen pal's answer, with photos of real letters.
- Offer group admins a free founding subscription so they can judge it for themselves. Don't ask them to promote it.
- Attend one or two local home-ed meet-ups or fairs with printed sample letters.
- **Metric:** sign-ups by source (each group gets its own link).

### 3. Creators (mostly the cost of free letters, from late October)

- Pick **15 micro-creators** (5,000-50,000 followers) in home education, screen-free parenting, reading and history for kids.
- Gift each one 3 months of Pen Pal, and give them a personal promotion code (10% off for their audience, plus 15% commission for them).
- Ask them for an honest reel of their child opening a letter. Never script it.
- **Cost:** roughly £110 in letter costs, plus commission.

### 4. Christmas gift guides and press (start this week: deadlines are now)

- **National gift guides close in October and early November.** Pitch this week to parenting sites and print or online gift guides (Mumsnet, Netmums, family and education titles, regional papers).
- **Story angles:**
  - "Children are writing letters again, to pen pals who lived 200 years ago."
  - The founder's story.
  - Later, a data story: "X% of children wrote back."
- Local press and BBC local radio love a human story, especially one with a local link. For example, a child in Dorset writing to Mary Anning.
- **Press kit:** the sample letters, 5 photos, the opening video, the founder's bio, and a short FAQ on AI and safety.

### 5. Heritage partners (start conversations in November, results in spring)

- Pen pals with a "home" museum:
  - **Lyme Regis Museum** (Mary Anning's birthplace)
  - **Grace Darling Museum** (RNLI, Bamburgh)
  - **SS Great Britain** (Brunel, Bristol)
  - **Down House** (Darwin, English Heritage)
  - **Shakespeare's Birthplace / Shakespeare's Globe**
- Possible offers: a co-branded "letter from…" card in their shop or newsletter, a revenue share, or a pen pal letter for their visitors.

### 6. Paid social (Meta) (test from 16 November)

- **Start at £10 a day** with two audiences:
  - parents of 5 to 12-year-olds interested in home education, educational toys or reading;
  - grandparents aged 55+, for gifts.
- **Creative:** the opening video, close-ups of letters ("Dear Oliver…"), and stationery carousels (one card per pen pal).
- **UK benchmarks** to compare against (vendor data): CPM about £6-9, CPC about £1. Costs peak in late December, so **spend in Black Friday week and early December, not just before Christmas**.
- **Rules:** stop any ad set costing more than £35 per sale after £100 of spend. Double any set under £20.

### 7. Search and content (small now, compounds later)

- Google Ads with a small budget, on phrases like "history subscription for kids UK", "gift for 7 year old who loves dinosaurs", "letter subscription for children".
- **Search content:** one page per pen pal for KS1/KS2 topics (e.g. "Mary Anning for kids"), each with a free printable sample letter and Real history card. Collect email addresses for the free printables.

### 8. Email

- **Waitlist sequence:** welcome → sample letter → "how replies work" → founding offer → last call.
- **Customers:** a short monthly "news from the pen pals" email (only to those who opted in, with the unsubscribe link built in). Ask for a referral after the first reply.

## 9. What we measure

**Funnel:** visit → sign-up → paid → first letter → **child writes back** → still subscribed at month 3.

**Every Friday, in one sheet:**

- **New paid subscribers** by source, and **cost per paid subscriber** by channel.
- **Conversion:** visit to paid (aim for 2-3%).
- **Reply rate** for Pen Pal children (aim for at least 40%).
- **Cancellations**, and the reasons people give.
- **Letter quality:** letters rejected in review, and changes parents ask for.
- **Safeguarding:** held replies, and how quickly each was handled (same day).

**Tools:**

- Stripe for revenue and cancellations.
- `/admin` for letters and replies.
- A cookie-free visitor counter (GoatCounter or Plausible).
- A unique promotion code or link for each channel and creator.

## 10. Operations during launch

- **Review time:** about 2-3 minutes a letter. 100 Pen Pal families is about 200 letters, or **8-10 hours a month**. Plan for twice that in the first month while we learn.
- **Safeguarding over Christmas:** agree a cover rota for 20 December to 3 January. Someone must check held replies every day.
- **Christmas posting:** post December letters early (by about 11 December). Gifts start in January.
- **Customer support:** one inbox, answered within a working day. Draft replies to the common questions in section 3.

## 11. Risks and how we handle them

| Risk | What we do |
|---|---|
| Backlash against AI | Be open from the first sentence and show real letters. People check every letter. Never overclaim |
| A letter with a mistake goes public | Review every letter at launch. Thank the person who spotted it, correct it, and send the family a corrected letter |
| Safeguarding incident | Written policy, a trained lead and deputy, the same-day rule, holiday cover, records |
| Christmas post delays | Gift certificates (first letter in January); December letters posted early |
| Low reply rates | Better P.S. questions. A "has your child written back?" email to parents after 10 days (January build). Showcase replies (with permission) |
| Cancellations once the novelty wears off | Story arcs, pen pals rotating every three letters, and replies that get answered |
| Ad costs spike in December | Spend earlier, put organic and creator channels first, keep a strict cost-per-sale cut-off |
| Too much demand for our review time | Cap founding families at 100. Turn off `REVIEW_ALL` only once quality is proven |
