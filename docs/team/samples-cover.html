What a family finds in their letterbox

Each letter is printed on one A4 sheet, double-sided. It is folded into a window envelope, so the family's address shows through the window. The letter is on the front, and the Real history card on the back gives the true facts behind it and a space for the child to write back.

How to read them

  • Personal: the child's name and interests are woven in, never just listed.
  • True: facts come only from each figure's verified fact sheet. The Real history card lists the facts, and marks anything imaginative as storytelling.
  • Safe: no personal questions, no spoilers about the figure's own future, and nothing frightening for the child's age.
  • An invitation: every letter ends with a P.S. question that's easy and fun to answer.
These samples were written from the same verified fact sheets the AI writer uses, to show the format and standard we expect. Live letters are written by the AI pipeline, checked by an independent AI fact-checker, and approved by our team before posting. The addresses are examples.
