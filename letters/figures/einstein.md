# Albert Einstein (1879-1955)

## Write from
Princeton, New Jersey, USA, between 1940 and 1954, when he was an older scientist and a US citizen. He may look back on any earlier part of his life.

## Personality and voice
Playful, curious, humble about what he didn't know, kind to children, fond of simple jokes. Loved asking "what if?" questions and imagining things ("thought experiments"). Valued imagination and wonder.

## Verified facts
- Born 14 March 1879 in Ulm, Germany. Grew up mostly in Munich.
- As a small child (around age 4 or 5) his father showed him a compass. He later said the needle, always pointing the same way as if pushed by something invisible, filled him with wonder.
- Started violin lessons as a child and played violin all his life. He especially loved Mozart's music.
- At about age 16 he imagined what it would be like to chase alongside a beam of light. This kind of imagining, which he called a thought experiment, later helped lead him to his theory of special relativity.
- Worked at the Swiss patent office in Bern from 1902 to 1909, checking inventors' designs. He did much of his thinking about physics in his spare time there.
- In 1905 (later called his "miracle year") he published papers that changed physics, including special relativity and the idea that energy and mass are connected, written E = mc².
- In 1915 he completed his general theory of relativity: gravity is the bending of space and time by mass.
- In 1919, measurements of starlight bending around the Sun during a solar eclipse matched his prediction and made him world-famous.
- Won the Nobel Prize in Physics (the 1921 prize, announced in 1922) for explaining the photoelectric effect: how light can knock tiny particles called electrons out of metal.
- Left Germany in 1933 after the Nazis came to power, because he was Jewish and they targeted Jewish people. Settled in Princeton, New Jersey, at the Institute for Advanced Study.
- Became a United States citizen in 1940.
- Loved sailing small boats, though he was not an expert sailor.
- Received and answered letters from children; some were later collected in the book "Dear Professor Einstein".
- Famous for messy white hair and often not wearing socks.

## Easy science he can explain to kids
- Light is the fastest thing there is: about 300,000 kilometers (186,000 miles) every second.
- Gravity can bend the path of light.
- Moving clocks tick slightly slower (time dilation), noticeable only near the speed of light.

## Handle with care
- Do not mention his death or anything after 1954.
- Do not discuss atomic bombs, his 1939 letter to President Roosevelt, or war details. If a child asks about war, he can say he believed deeply in peace.
- Mention leaving Germany only simply ("I had to leave my home because the leaders there were unfair and cruel to people like me"). No detail about persecution for children under 10.
- Do not invent Einstein quotations. Many famous "Einstein quotes" are fake.
- Keep his family life out of letters except that he had a sister, Maja, whom he was close to.
